SpeakRx Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpeakRx (the “App”), an alarm and spoken reminder app, handles information. The App is provided by its developer, who can be contacted at chengysur@gmail.com.

Information the App handles

The App lets you create alarms and reminders. Alarm settings can include the alarm time, repeat days, reminder text, duration, and sound volume. These settings are stored locally on your device so the App can display and schedule your alarms.

If you choose to enable the optional app password feature, the password and its enabled/disabled setting are stored using the device's secure storage facility.

How information is used and shared

The App uses alarm information to schedule and play your alarms, display your reminder text, and provide optional password protection. The App does not send your alarm settings, reminder text, or optional app password to the developer. The App does not include advertising or an account system.

When an alarm speaks your reminder, the App uses the text-to-speech capability provided by your device. The device's operating system, selected speech engine, and their settings govern how speech is processed. Some speech engines may process text on-device; others may use a service provided by the device or speech-engine provider. Please consult that provider's privacy information for details. The App developer does not receive the reminder text through this speech feature.

Permissions and device functions

Depending on your device and settings, the App may use notification permission to show alarm notifications, exact-alarm permission to schedule alarms at the selected time, and audio playback to sound and speak an alarm. Alarm scheduling and notifications are managed on your device. The App does not request microphone access.

Storage and deletion

Alarm settings and the optional password are kept on your device, not in an account with the developer. You can delete alarm settings in the App. You can remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App. Device backups and operating-system services are subject to your device and account settings.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose alarm and reminder utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App's data practices or features change. The current version will be published at the privacy-policy URL shown on the App's Google Play listing.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact chengysur@gmail.com.
